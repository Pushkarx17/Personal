Privacy Policy

This is a personal portfolio site. It has no accounts, no forms, no shopping basket, no comments and no analytics. Nothing about you is collected by me directly, and nothing is stored on your device.

Last updated 13 September 2026.

Who is responsible

The data controller for this site is Pushkar Kodigehalli Umesh, an individual based in Nottingham, United Kingdom. You can reach me at pushkar.uk@outlook.com.

What this site collects

Directly, nothing. There is no form to submit, no newsletter to join, no login, and no tracking script of any kind. The site does not use Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom, advertising pixels or any comparable tool.

What does happen is ordinary web serving. To send you this page, my host has to receive a request from your device, and it keeps short-lived records of those requests for security and reliability:

Data
IP address, user-agent string, the URL requested, timestamp, and approximate country — the standard contents of a web server log.
Who holds it
Cloudflare, Inc., which hosts this site on Cloudflare Pages and sits in front of it as a CDN and DDoS filter. I do not have access to a per-visitor log and I do not export, analyse or retain one.
Why
Legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR: delivering the page you asked for, and keeping the site available and protected from abuse. There is no marketing or profiling purpose.
How long
Governed by Cloudflare's own retention schedule, not mine. Their edge logs are typically retained for a matter of days.
Where
Cloudflare is a US company operating a global network, so this data may be processed outside the UK. Those transfers are covered by Cloudflare's standard contractual clauses and UK addendum.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies at all, and writes nothing to localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB. Because nothing is stored on or read from your device beyond what is strictly necessary to transmit the page, no consent banner is required under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. The cookie policy sets this out in full.

Fonts are served from this site

The two typefaces this site uses are hosted here rather than loaded from Google Fonts. That is deliberate: a webfont fetched from fonts.gstatic.com would disclose your IP address to Google on every page view, without your knowledge and with no way to refuse it short of blocking the request. Self-hosting removes that entirely.

The result is that loading this page contacts no third-party server whatsoever. Every image, stylesheet, script and font comes from pushkarku.com.

Links to other sites

The site links out to LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Maps and Geospatial Ventures. Those links are plain hyperlinks — no embedded widgets, no iframes, no share buttons — so nothing is sent to any of them unless you actually click through. Once you do, you are on their site and their privacy policy applies, not mine.

If you email me

My email address is published on this site so that people can contact me. If you write to me, I will hold your message and address for as long as it is useful to have done so, on the basis of legitimate interests in replying to correspondence. My mailbox is provided by Microsoft (Outlook). I will not add you to any mailing list or pass your details to anyone else.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, and to ask me to correct it, erase it, restrict how it is used, or object to my using it — along with the right to data portability. In practice I hold nothing about visitors to this site, so a request will usually only be meaningful if you have emailed me.

There is no automated decision-making or profiling on this site.

To exercise any of these rights, email pushkar.uk@outlook.com. If you are unhappy with how I have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk (opens in a new tab) or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

This site is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If I add anything to this site that changes the picture above — an analytics tool, a contact form, an embedded video — I will update this page and the date at the top before it goes live.
